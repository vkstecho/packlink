APP NAME (27/30)
PackLink: Packaging Network

SHORT DESCRIPTION (68/80)
Jobs, chat & directory for India's flexible packaging professionals.

FULL DESCRIPTION (2824/4000)
PackLink is the members-only professional network built exclusively for India's flexible packaging industry. Connect with verified colleagues, find your next job, post openings and build a resume, all in one app.

WHO IS IT FOR?
People who work in film manufacturing, converting, printing, lamination, slitting, metallizing and packaging plants: operators, engineers, supervisors, quality and maintenance teams, managers, HR and plant heads.

KEY FEATURES

💬 Industry Group Chat
• Talk with verified packaging peers, not random strangers from the open web
• Share text and photos, react to messages and search the conversation
• Ask technical questions, share process tips and stay updated on the industry
• Optional notifications so you don't miss important messages

👥 Member Directory
• Find professionals by name, company, designation or department
• Filter by designation to reach the right people quickly
• See who is online and who was active recently
• View profiles with company, role, experience and a short bio

💼 Job Board
• Browse openings posted by people inside the industry
• Search by title, company or location, or view recent posts from the last 7 days
• Post your own job in a few taps, with company, location, description and contact number
• Call or WhatsApp the recruiter directly, and tap "I'm interested" to save a job
• Manage and delete your own posts any time

📄 Resume Tools
• Build an ATS-friendly resume designed for packaging professionals
• Choose from ready templates and export a PDF
• Add machines operated, technical skills, education and work history

👤 Your Profile
• Add your photo, bio, company, designation, department and experience
• Set your target designation and target salary
• Light and dark themes to suit your eyes

WHY PACKLINK?
✔ Admin-verified members only: every mobile number is approved, so you network with real industry professionals
✔ Simple, secure sign-in with a one-time password (OTP) sent to your mobile number
✔ Built for the shop floor and the boardroom alike: fast, lightweight and easy to use
✔ Free for members

YOUR PRIVACY
• We don't sell your data or show ads
• Sensitive details such as salary and email are visible only to you and the admin
• You can request deletion of your account and data at any time

HOW TO JOIN
PackLink is open to approved members. Open the app and sign in with your mobile number. If your number is not yet registered, send the admin a WhatsApp message with your current employment proof and resume, and you will be approved after verification.
